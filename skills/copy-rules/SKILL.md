# Copy rules

All text on the site must read like a person explaining a product to a colleague. Short, plain, direct.

## Do

- Say what the product is and does in the first sentence of each section.
- Use concrete nouns: "machine", "GPU", "model", "data", "electricity".
- Keep sentences under 20 words. Use periods and commas. Use a colon when listing.
- Use active voice. "You buy the machine once." not "The machine is purchased once."
- Write "you" and "your" for the customer.
- Keep headings short, 2 to 5 words, in sentence case.
- Name the real trade-off when there is one. Example: open models are a little behind the frontier, but close enough for most work.

## Do not

- No em dashes and no en dashes. Use a comma, period or colon.
- No hype words: revolutionize, unleash, seamless, cutting-edge, next-generation, empower, elevate, supercharge, game-changing, leverage, robust, transform, unlock, harness, journey, world-class, state-of-the-art.
- Do not start a sentence with "Imagine". Do not end a section with a question.
- No exclamation marks.
- No emojis.
- No triplets of vague adjectives ("fast, secure, powerful").
- No claims with numbers that are not in AGENTS.md. No prices, speeds, benchmarks, customer names, quotes, awards.
- Do not say "AI-powered", "AI-driven" or "in today's fast-paced world".

## Before and after

Bad: "Unleash the power of cutting-edge AI with our seamless, robust solutions."
Good: "We sell a machine that runs an AI model in your office. You buy it once."

Bad: "Imagine a world where your data never leaves your building."
Good: "Your data stays in your building. Nothing is sent to an outside API."

Bad: "Our game-changing pricing model empowers businesses."
Good: "You pay once for the hardware. After that you pay for electricity."

## Fixed copy

- Tagline: "Stop renting your intelligence. Own it."
- Button: "Book a call"
- Footer: "WarpDancer" and the current year.

## Check

Search the whole file for these before you finish:

- the characters for an em dash and an en dash
- every word from the banned list above
- "LIVE", "NEW", "BETA"
- any number that is not in AGENTS.md

Fix every hit and search again.
